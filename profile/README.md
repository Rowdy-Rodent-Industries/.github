## About Us

**Rowdy Rodent Industries (RRI)** is a community-driven development organization focused on creating custom armor, equipment, content, and more!

Our development efforts cover a variety of disciplines, including:

- **3D Modeling & Texturing** — Custom models, textures, and assets.
- **Configuration Development** — Weapons, vehicles, equipment, and factions.
- **Scripting & Systems** — SQF scripting, gameplay mechanics, and functionality.
- **Tools & Utilities** — Resources to support mod development.
- **Documentation** — Tutorials, guides, and development standards.
